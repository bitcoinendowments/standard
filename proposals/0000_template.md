## 1. Motivation

What problem exists today. Name the clause and describe the situation a deployment actually runs into. A motivation that only describes an improvement in elegance is not a motivation.

## 2. Specification

The exact text to add, remove, or change, written as it would appear in BES 0001, including the verification label for every new or altered clause. New clauses take the next unused identifier. Existing identifiers are never renumbered or reused.

## 3. Rationale

Why this wording rather than the alternatives considered. Name the alternatives and say why each was set aside.

## 4. Impact on existing deployments

Whether a deployment conformant with the current release stays conformant. If not, this is a MAJOR change under `../VERSIONING.md` and this section must state exactly what an existing deployment has to do.

## 5. Security and legal considerations

What this makes safer, what it makes riskier, and what it moves from one verification label to another. Moving a claim from GOVERNANCE to BITCOIN is a strong claim and needs to be shown, not asserted.

## 6. Who is worse off if this is adopted

Reviewers read this section first.

Name the organizations, situations, or existing deployments this excludes, burdens, or invalidates. Almost every tightening of a requirement makes someone previously conformant nonconformant, and almost every loosening weakens a claim someone was relying on. A proposal claiming nobody is worse off is usually one that has not been thought through, and it will be sent back.

## Decision

Recorded by the maintainers when the comment period closes. Includes the outcome, the reasoning, and any dissent, stated by the person who held it.
