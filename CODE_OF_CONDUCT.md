# Code of conduct

Short version: argue about the work, not the person.

**Expected.** Technical disagreement, blunt review, and specific objections. Reasons instead of authority. Recorded dissent. Asking a basic question without apology, since this project is read by lawyers who do not know Bitcoin and by engineers who do not know charity governance, and both are welcome here.

**Not accepted.** Personal attacks, harassment, contempt for someone's expertise or lack of it, and persistent bad faith after a point has been answered.

Maintainers may close a thread, reject a contribution, or block an account. Every such action is taken in public and with a stated reason, except where stating the reason would expose a person to harm.

If forking is ever the better answer, the license already permits it without permission or ill will.
